REAL WORKFLOWS · PRODUCT INSIGHTS Team project experience / Product discovery

Real operational problems.
Reusable product building blocks.

ARZ AI studies the workflows behind manual entry, coordination and fragmented tools. These are project scenarios and engineering lessons from our team, informing the configurable SaaS products we are building.

FIELD NOTE 01Sports venues · LINE booking

How do venues avoid double bookings across phone, front desk and LINE?

Sports facilities may rely on phone and in-person reservations plus a legacy management system. A new booking channel must account for venue rules, special opening hours and staff review.

Workflow challenges explored

  • A LINE booking entry point for selecting spaces, dates and available time slots.
  • Scheduling rules for booking durations, buffers, closures and exceptional business hours.
  • Simultaneous booking conflicts, administrator approval and legacy system handoff.
Reusable SaaS capabilities being developed
Resource schedulingConflict checksApproval workflowsLegacy integration
FIELD NOTE 02Temple services · Forms and orders

Can handwritten signups, payment records and prayer documents share a single flow?

Traditional temple services involve collecting attendee information, checking payments and preparing documents, often across paper forms and disconnected messages.

Workflow challenges explored

  • A LINE entry point and online forms for collecting required registration details.
  • Centralized submission, payment reference and order status tracking for staff.
  • Data-assisted document preparation to reduce repetitive transcription.

Configurable formsOrder trackingPayment workflowsDocument generation

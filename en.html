FAQ

A few good questions.

Who is the appointment product for?

Businesses, studios, and service providers managing scheduled appointments. Suitability depends on operating hours, appointment rules, capacity, and existing workflows.

Can I create a real booking on this website?

The interactive example is a local front-end demonstration. It does not connect to a real booking backend. Please contact us for onboarding or product availability.

Does ARZ AI support other processes?

We discuss workflow automation for intake, notifications, data handling, integrations, and appropriate AI-assisted workflows. Specific implementation depends on the business requirements.

How is Claude or AI part of the roadmap?

We are exploring AI-supported intake and natural-language assistance for administrative workflows. Actions affecting appointments would follow business rules and confirmation steps. Specific AI features are planned or evaluated separately from the launched booking product.
